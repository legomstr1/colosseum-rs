Round 4

Stage Bench, iterated: dice show Roman numerals (I–III, IV, V, VI), programs carry their numbers everywhere (the programs list, your seat, opponents' pills, produce cards), your seat is wider and lists your programs by name, and hovering an opponent shows theirs. Trading has its own stage in five states.

Round 3

Built from Phase Stage (10), with more room and fewer words. The spaces are square, and the ring picks whichever shape (10×10 up to 14×6) gives the biggest squares in the space it has. The middle shows only what the current phase needs: icons, dice with pips, and program cards that highlight the score you'd get now. Use the dark pill in the bottom-right corner to switch between 3, 4 and 5 players, and click a phase in the tracker to preview its stage.

11 · Stage and Bench seats underneath

Wide ring with all players on an equal bench below it. With 5 players the bench just gets narrower seats.

12 · Stage and Column seats on the right

Seats stacked on the right, so the ring keeps its height and turns squarer (11×9). More players means shorter seats, not a smaller board.

13 · Open Floor light us versus them

Your seat and programs on the left; opponents as chips in the top bar (hover for their seat). The ring gets the most space of the three.

Round 2

From the feedback on round 1: light mode only on the tan table from Tabletop; the round and phase tracker from Night HUD; the program list from Ledger without search, showing every spectator fall-off step; player tokens always drawn one tile each; rectangular boards that fill the width; and the board itself takes clicks. In every one, pick a die, click a noble to see its moves, or click a faded noble on a dashed square to move it.
